Common questions

Which platforms do you monitor? +
We monitor Funda, Kamernet, and HousingAnywhere simultaneously across 19 cities. More platforms are being added.
How fast are the alerts? +
As soon as a listing appears that matches your filters, you get a Telegram notification. We run continuous monitoring 24/7. Most alerts arrive within minutes of a listing going live.
What is the Application Score? +
A personal 0-100 score calculated for every listing based on income vs rent, contract type, document readiness, and timing. It tells you how strong your application is and what to fix first.
What is the AI Rental Assistant? +
A 3-in-1 tool you open from any alert. Write Letter generates a personalised application letter in seconds. Viewing Tips prepares you with specific questions and a room-by-room inspection checklist. Negotiation assesses the market and gives you word-for-word scripts.
Does it work for rentals and purchases? +
Yes. Filter by rental, purchase, or both. Your Application Score and tips adapt to the listing type, and all tools cover both journeys.
