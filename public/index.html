FAQ

Common questions.

Which platforms do you monitor?
We monitor Funda, Kamernet, and HousingAnywhere simultaneously — covering the vast majority of Dutch rental listings across 19 cities. More platforms are being added.
How fast are the alerts?
As soon as a new listing appears on Funda, Kamernet, or HousingAnywhere that matches your filters. We run continuous monitoring cycles — 24/7, without interruption.
Does it work for both rentals and purchases?
Yes. Filter by rental, purchase, or both. Your Application Score and tips adapt to the listing type.
What is the Application Score?
A personal 0–100 score calculated for every listing based on 5 pillars: income vs rent, your contract type, document readiness, listing timing, and competition level. It tells you how strong your application is — and what to fix first.
What are the Smart Tips?
For every alert, we read the listing description and detect what the landlord is looking for — income requirements, tenant preferences, property conditions. You get up to 5 specific, actionable tips tailored to that listing. Not generic advice.
What is the AI Letter and Application Package?
Tap "AI Letter" in any Telegram alert to get a personalised motivation letter generated in seconds — based on the listing, the landlord's requirements, and your profile. For a complete package, open the web app from the alert: it generates a motivation letter, personal introduction, WhatsApp one-liner, and a financial summary card all at once. You always send the application yourself — HomeSeeker never contacts landlords on your behalf.
How do I cancel?
Send /cancel to the HomeSeeker bot in Telegram, or use the link in your confirmation email. Cancel before day 7 and you pay nothing at all.
