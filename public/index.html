FAQ

Common questions.

Which platforms are monitored?
We currently monitor Funda (koop + huur) — Pararius, HousingAnywhere and more coming soon.
How fast do I get notified?
New listings are checked every 10 minutes, 24/7. Most home seekers receive alerts within 5 minutes.
What is the chance score?
A personal 0–100 score based on your income vs the listing price, document readiness, and price fit. Helps you prioritise what to respond to first.
Does it work for koop and huur?
Yes. You can filter by koop, huur, or both. The chance and deal scores adapt accordingly.
What is the AI motivation letter?
Tap "AI Letter" in Telegram, choose a style, answer 3 quick questions, and get a personalised letter to copy, email, or send via WhatsApp. You always send it yourself.
How do I cancel?
Send /stop to the HomeSeeker bot, or use the link in your confirmation email. Cancel before day 7 and you pay nothing.
