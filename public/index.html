FAQ

Questions, answered

Which platforms does HomeSeeker monitor?▾

Funda, Kamernet and HousingAnywhere today, with more source platforms added on an ongoing basis — 24/7 across 19 Dutch cities, for both renting and buying. New listings reach your Telegram the moment they go live.

Why Telegram and not email?▾

Telegram keeps new listings completely separate from your personal email and WhatsApp, so an alert never gets buried between other messages. It also delivers instantly the moment a listing goes live, and takes under 2 minutes to connect — no separate account needed.

What does the Application Score mean?▾

It reflects how well your profile fits a specific listing — income, contract, documents, timing and competition. It's about your fit, not a guarantee of getting the home.

Who is Scout?▾

Scout is your AI assistant. It reads each listing, tells you what the landlord wants, writes your letter, preps you for viewings and coaches you through negotiation — and it gets sharper the more the platform is used.

How is this different from a normal alert service?▾

Most services stop at the notification. HomeSeeker takes you from alert to signed contract — with guidance at every step, not just a link to a listing everyone else already saw.

Can I cancel anytime?▾

Yes. Start with a 7-day free trial and cancel whenever you like — no lock-in, set up in under 2 minutes.
