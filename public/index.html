FAQ

Common questions.

Which platforms are monitored?
We currently monitor Funda — the platform where the vast majority of Dutch listings appear. We are expanding to Pararius, HousingAnywhere and more.
How fast do I get notified?
New listings are checked continuously, 24/7. We send an instant alert once we have a match.
Does it work for both rentals and purchases?
Yes. Filter by rental, purchase, or both. Scores adapt accordingly.
What is the application strength?
A personal 0–100 score based on your income vs the price, document readiness, and price fit. Helps you prioritise which listings to respond to first.
What is the Application Letter?
Tap "Application Letter" in Telegram, pick a style, answer 3 questions, and get a ready-to-send letter. Copy it, email it, or send via WhatsApp. You always send it yourself.
How do I cancel?
Send /stop to the HomeSeeker bot, or use the link in your confirmation email. Cancel before day 7 and you pay nothing.
