FAQ

Common questions.

Which platforms are monitored?
We currently monitor Funda — with Pararius, HousingAnywhere and more being added soon.
How fast do I get notified?
Within minutes of a new listing going live. We check for new listings every 10 minutes around the clock.
What is the chance score?
A personal 0–100 score calculated from your gross income (compared to the rent or mortgage), your document readiness, and how the listing price compares to your maximum. It helps you prioritise which listings to respond to first.
Can I filter by city and type?
Yes. You can set your preferred city, koop or huur (or both), price range, number of rooms, minimum area, energy label, and more. You can also set a minimum chance score so you only receive alerts above your threshold.
What is the AI motivation letter?
When you tap "Generate Application Letter" in Telegram, the bot asks 4 quick questions (name, income, move-in date, extra info) and generates a professional Dutch motivation letter addressed to that specific landlord or agent. Copy and send it directly — you do the sending, so you can personalise it first.
How do I cancel?
You can cancel anytime before the trial ends via the link in your confirmation email, or by sending /stop to the HomeSeeker bot on Telegram. You will not be charged if you cancel before day 7.
