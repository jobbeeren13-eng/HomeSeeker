Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who we are

HomeSeeker is operated as an independent service that provides real-time housing alerts and AI-powered application tools for people searching for housing in the Netherlands. For questions about your data, contact support@homeseeker.dev.

2. What data we collect and why

  • Email address — to create and manage your account, and to send service emails. Legal basis: performance of contract.
  • Name — to personalise your experience and AI-generated content. Legal basis: performance of contract.
  • Income, employment type, and housing preferences — to calculate your Application Score and generate personalised advice. Legal basis: performance of contract.
  • Telegram chat ID — to send you property alerts via Telegram. Legal basis: performance of contract.
  • Payment data — processed entirely by Stripe. We do not store card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to all payment processing.
  • IP address and usage data — collected automatically for security and service reliability. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

3. How long we keep your data

  • Active subscribers — data is kept for the duration of your subscription.
  • Cancelled subscribers — data is deleted within 90 days of cancellation.
  • Payment records — kept for 7 years as required by Dutch tax law (Belastingdienst).

4. Processors we share your data with

We use the following processors to provide the service. Each processes only the data needed for its role, under a data processing agreement with us. The location and transfer safeguard for each is listed below.

  • Railway (web app, bot & primary database) — stores and processes your full account data: email, name, income, employment type, housing preferences and Telegram chat ID. Hosted by a US-based provider; data may be processed in the United States. Transfer safeguard: EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
  • Hetzner (listing scraper & off-site database backup) — holds a backup copy of your account data. Servers located in Germany (EU/EEA). Hetzner's privacy policy.
  • Anthropic (AI response generation) — receives your name, profile data (income, employment type, housing preferences) and listing context inside the prompts used to generate letters and advice. Based in the United States; data processed in the US. Transfer safeguard: EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Stripe (payment processing) — handles your name, email and payment data. We never receive or store card details. Based in the United States; data processed in the US. Transfer safeguard: EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Resend (transactional email) — receives your name and email address to send service emails (welcome, trial and cancellation notices). Based in the United States; data processed in the US. Transfer safeguard: EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Resend's privacy policy.
  • Telegram (alert delivery) — receives your Telegram chat ID and the content of the alerts we send you. Processing is also governed by Telegram's own privacy policy.

Some of these providers are based in or process data in the United States. Where your data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA), we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses as the legal safeguard. You can request a copy of the relevant safeguards by emailing support@homeseeker.dev.

We do not sell your data to third parties.

5. Your rights under AVG/GDPR

  • Right to access — request a copy of your data by emailing support@homeseeker.dev.
  • Right to correction — update incorrect data via /filters in Telegram.
  • Right to deletion — email support@homeseeker.dev to delete your account and all associated data.
  • Right to object — you can object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

6. Cookies

HomeSeeker does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. No cookie consent banner is required.

7. Data security

Your data is stored on encrypted infrastructure. We use HTTPS for all data transmission. Sensitive data such as income and profile details is not shared beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

8. Changes to this policy

We will notify active subscribers of material changes to this policy via Telegram or email.
