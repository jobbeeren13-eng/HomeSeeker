Erfpacht Checker

Ground lease analysis for Dutch property buyers. Erfpacht is the single biggest hidden risk for expats - understand your terms before you bid.

What is erfpacht? When you buy a property with erfpacht, you buy the building but not the land beneath it. You pay an annual fee (canon) to the land owner - usually the municipality. The canon can increase dramatically at revision points, sometimes doubling or tripling your annual cost. Some banks refuse to lend on certain erfpacht properties. Erfpacht is common in Amsterdam Centrum, IJburg, Watergraafsmeer, parts of Den Haag, Leiden, and Eindhoven.
