Rental Application Documents

Have everything ready before you start applying. Landlords move fast - being able to send all documents the same day dramatically improves your chances.

Prepare all required documents before your first viewing. Having them ready to send within the hour is one of the strongest signals you can give a landlord.

Identity

Every landlord requires this.

0 of 2 ready
  • Passport or EU ID card Required
    Clear photo or scan of the information page. Must be valid (not expired).
  • BSN number (if you have one) Helps
    Your Dutch citizen service number. Not always required but signals you are registered in the Netherlands.

Income Proof

Most landlords require 3x to 4x the monthly rent in gross income. Choose the documents that match your situation.

0 of 5 ready
  • Last 3 months payslips Required
    In Dutch or German ideally. If your payslips are in another language, prepare a certified translation or official employer letter in Dutch or English.
  • Employer statement (werkgeversverklaring) Required
    Letter from your employer confirming your contract type (permanent or temporary), start date, and gross monthly salary. This is a standard document - ask your HR team. Download the standard NVM form if your employer asks for a template.
  • Last 3 months bank statements Required
    Shows consistent salary deposits and no large unexplained debits. Export as PDF from your bank's app.
  • IB-60 / Annual income statement Freelancers
    For ZZP (self-employed): your last 3 annual tax returns (jaaropgave) from the Belastingdienst. Log in to MijnBelastingdienst to download.
  • 30% ruling decision letter If applicable
    If you have the Dutch 30% tax ruling, some landlords accept your gross income before the deduction for the income calculation - attach the decision letter.

References

Optional but strongly recommended - especially for expats.

0 of 2 ready
  • Reference from previous landlord Recommended
    A short letter or email from your previous landlord confirming you paid rent on time and left the property in good condition. Ask for it before your current lease ends.
  • Employer reference contact Recommended
    Name and phone number of your HR contact or manager who can confirm your employment if the landlord calls for verification.

Extras That Help

0 of 3 ready
  • Motivation letter Highly recommended
    A short personal letter (under 200 words) explaining who you are and why you want this specific property. Use HomeSeeker's AI Letter generator.
  • Brief introduction paragraph Helpful
    3-4 sentences about yourself: job, lifestyle, why this location. Include in your application email. Easy to personalise with HomeSeeker's Full Application Package.
  • Photo (professional headshot optional) Optional
    Some applicants include a professional photo to make the application feel more personal. Entirely optional and not required.
